Dear HENNGE Engineering Talent Acquisition Team,

I am writing to apply for the \textbf{HENNGE Global Internship Program (GIP), Full-Stack Engineering Pathway}, at your Shibuya office in Tokyo. The chance to work alongside HENNGE's Cloud Product Development engineers in a global, English-speaking environment, and to strengthen my fundamentals through direct mentorship and hands-on AWS training, is exactly where I want to grow next.

What drew me to HENNGE specifically is the company's origin: a name shaped by the words for transformation and challenge. That posture, embracing change while continually challenging yourself, is the same one I try to build my engineering practice around. HENNGE's active contribution to Open Source Software, the international team, and the emphasis on learning culture over short-term output describe the environment where I believe I will do my best work.

I am currently a Software Engineering Intern at Headrest, an early-stage startup, shipping the Android client of our mobile app, extending Python backend services, and building AI-driven content-automation pipelines that cross-post to Twitter, Substack, Reddit, and Instagram. Earlier I delivered a production multilingual recruitment platform (live at \href{https://ii-msc.com}{ii-msc.com}) as a paid freelance engagement, in Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS with i18n across four languages. My CodeMotion project taught me decisions HENNGE values: isolating untrusted, model-generated Python in a Dockerized sandbox, orchestrating a FastAPI backend, and integrating Supabase auth with a Next.js client.

Two things the CV does not fully show. I genuinely enjoy contributing to open source: I have two merged contributions to Impeccable, a design skill library for AI coding harnesses with over 23,000 GitHub stars. I also like leading from the front, having co-led a five-person team to ship SeaGuard, an AI mobile assistant that won the Most Product Readiness award at Hackcelestial 2.0 from a field of more than 400 participants.

I am proficient in Python and TypeScript with React, comfortable in Unix-like environments, and hold an AWS Academy Cloud Foundations certification. I am eager to build a long-term career in Japan, and the GIP is the right way to begin. I will take ownership of my code, explain my trade-offs clearly, and treat the program as a space to grow into the engineer HENNGE expects me to become.

Thank you for considering my application.
